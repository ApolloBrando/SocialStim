# Private Mafia knowledge and succession

Mafia members receive allied identities and actual roles separately from public claims/reveals. The player sees a private starting team message and teammate role annotations in the roster. After each night, all Mafia NPCs receive a detached history of their faction's actual orders, results and visits, including passing and blocked attempts. A Mafia player also receives private transcript entries and an ordinary (non-debug) Mafia log. Town and Neutral strategies/views receive no team records or allied role map.

An NPC's own belief model can use a known allied visit to distrust a contradictory Lookout account. That is private evidence, not a public reveal of the reporter's true role. Team knowledge does not publish the faction's real actions to everyone or overwrite fake public reports.

If the team's last attacking member dies, exactly one living Mafia member inherits the catalogued attacking Mafia role (Mafioso in the shipped catalogue). Survivor seat order makes the choice deterministic. Night casualties are all applied before choosing a successor; nobody killed in the same resolution can inherit. An existing living Mafia killer prevents another promotion. No surviving Mafia or no attacking role definition means no promotion. Execution succession occurs before the night and endgame check, so the team retains a usable kill. The new role replaces its previous ability/resources and is shared privately with allies, including a promoted player. Other factions know the succession rule exists without learning the successor's identity.

Historical night records retain the role and action used during that night. Promotion cannot turn a previous Forger/Strongarm action into an invented Mafioso shot. Public death reveals keep the deceased's role; spectator roles show the promoted current role after the player dies.

Portable tests cover shared identities/actions and isolation, player night promotion, NPC execution promotion, actual attacks on a later night, simultaneous casualties, single-killer uniqueness, historical record stability and private Lookout contradictions. Full seeded simulations remain regression checks, not balance proof.
